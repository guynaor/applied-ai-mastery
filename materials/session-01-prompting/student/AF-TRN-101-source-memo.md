## Reported operating condition

The primary coolant pump operates at approximately 3,450 RPM with a reported fluid flow rate of 420 GPM.

During high-thermal-stress production cycles, drive-shaft vibration amplitude was reported to increase by approximately 28% compared with the normal-cycle baseline.

The mechanical seal has required replacement after roughly 450 operating hours in each of the last three maintenance intervals.

## Observations recorded by maintenance

- Leakage begins as intermittent moisture around the seal housing.
- Leakage progresses during long high-temperature runs.
- Operators report that the pump sounds louder near the end of those runs.
- No visible cracks were found in the housing during the most recent inspection.
- The replacement seals were supplied under the same part number as the original seal.
