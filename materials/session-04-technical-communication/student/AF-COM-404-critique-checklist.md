# AF-COM-404 — Presentation Critique Checklist

Review the completed deck without assuming that attractive design means the argument is sound.

## Narrative

- [ ] The requested decision is clear by slide 1.
- [ ] Each slide advances the argument rather than repeating earlier content.
- [ ] Each slide has one explicit takeaway.
- [ ] The final slide asks for a specific decision and next checkpoint.

## Evidence discipline

- [ ] Every numerical claim is traceable to the source notes.
- [ ] Estimates are labelled as estimates.
- [ ] Interest is not presented as validated demand.
- [ ] Planning dates are not presented as commitments.
- [ ] Unsupported performance, regulatory, or customer claims are absent.

## Visual communication

- [ ] Every visual has an explanatory purpose.
- [ ] Decorative images do not imply a finished product design.
- [ ] Tables and charts use comparable units and honest scales.
- [ ] Text remains readable at presentation distance.

## Audience and action

- [ ] Leadership can identify the benefit, uncertainty, cost exposure, and control plan.
- [ ] Risks are paired with mitigations or validation steps.
- [ ] Speaker notes contain useful detail without contradicting the slides.
