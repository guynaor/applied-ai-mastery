# AF-COM-401 — Presentation Brief

**Requested by:** Sarah Chen, Engineering Manager  
**Audience:** AquaForge leadership team  
**Decision required:** Approve, revise, or reject a limited AquaNode Mini pilot build  
**Presentation length:** Six slides, five minutes

## Required narrative

1. Why the pilot is being proposed now
2. What the pilot will test
3. What evidence currently supports it
4. What remains uncertain
5. Cost, schedule, and risk controls
6. Requested decision and next checkpoint

## Rules

- Use only the supplied source notes.
- Do not describe estimates as approved budgets or committed dates.
- Do not claim customer demand is validated.
- Label assumptions and unresolved questions visibly.
- Each slide must have one takeaway sentence.
- Every visual must explain, compare, sequence, or support a claim.
- Put detail in speaker notes rather than overcrowding slides.

## Deliverables

- Completed storyboard
- Six-slide presentation
- Speaker notes
- Completed critique checklist
- One revised slide with a short explanation of the change
