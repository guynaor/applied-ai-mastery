# AF-COM-402 — AquaNode Mini Pilot Source Notes

## Engineering note — Sarah Chen

- Current AquaNode enclosure is larger than needed for installations that require only pH, temperature, and one RS485 sensor.
- A smaller prototype could test enclosure layout, connector access, thermal behaviour, and installation time.
- No final enclosure geometry has been approved.
- The electronics team believes the existing controller board can be reused, but connector clearance has not been checked in a smaller housing.

## Operations note — Omar Haddad

- Workshop capacity could support a pilot of 12 units if final drawings and purchasing decisions are released by 2026-09-05.
- This is a planning estimate, not a committed production date.
- Two long-lead components may require substitutions: the polycarbonate enclosure and M12 cable glands.
- Assembly time is unknown. A rough planning allowance of 2.5 hours per unit is being used until a build trial is completed.

## Procurement note — Elena Rossi

- Preliminary component estimate: EUR 186 per unit, excluding engineering time, certification, packaging, and field support.
- The estimate uses current small-quantity supplier pricing and may change.
- No supplier quotations have been approved.
- A 10% material contingency would add approximately EUR 223 to a 12-unit pilot.

## Customer-success note — Rachel Stein

- Three customers have asked whether AquaForge offers a smaller monitoring unit.
- None has issued a purchase order or signed a pilot agreement.
- One customer could potentially host two pilot units, subject to management approval and installation conditions.
- The requests indicate interest, not validated demand.

## QA note — Priya Nair

- The pilot must not be presented as a released product.
- Required checks should include ingress-risk inspection, connector strain relief, thermal observation, installation checklist, and traceable issue logging.
- Environmental and regulatory requirements for a commercial release have not been assessed.
- A pilot exit review should define whether the concept proceeds, changes, or stops.

## Finance note — internal planning conversation

- Leadership has discussed a pilot ceiling of approximately EUR 5,000.
- No budget has been approved.
- The figure may need to cover materials, fabrication, travel, and contingency, but the included categories are not yet agreed.

## Known tension

Sales would like a visually polished concept for an upcoming customer meeting. Engineering and QA do not want the concept shown as a committed product or supported by unverified performance claims.
