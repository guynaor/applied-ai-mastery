## Planning rules

1. Treat rows marked `HARD` in the constraints file as immovable unless the contingency explicitly says otherwise.
2. Cluster candidate activities by zone before assigning times.
3. Include travel, parking, check-in, setup, meals, and note-taking time.
4. Do not schedule more than two high-focus technical activities in one day.
5. Keep at least one 60-minute flexible block on each full day.
6. Label every opening hour, travel time, reservation, and access condition that requires live verification.
7. Produce a primary itinerary and at least two contingency rules.

## Deliverables

- Completed itinerary CSV.
- Calendar-import CSV.
- One-paragraph rationale explaining the geographic clustering.

- A fallback plan for a two-hour arrival delay and for one unavailable site.
