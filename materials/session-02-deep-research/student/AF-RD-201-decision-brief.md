# AF-RD-201 — Research Decision Brief

**Department:** Research & Development  
**Requested by:** Sarah Chen, Engineering Manager  
**Decision date:** 2026-08-15  
**Subject:** Frame architecture for the AquaNode vibration-test stand

## Business decision

AquaForge must choose the default frame architecture for one new in-house test stand used for endurance and vibration testing of AquaNode assemblies.

The shortlisted options are:

- **Option A:** 4040-series aluminium T-slot extrusion
- **Option B:** welded structural-steel rectangular tubing

## Required operating envelope

- Test-article mass: up to 100 kg
- Dynamic excitation: up to 35 Hz during routine testing
- Maximum stand footprint: 1,200 mm × 800 mm
- Expected service life: 5 years
- The stand may need sensor, guard, and fixture changes several times per year
- The workshop can cut, drill, and assemble extrusion internally
- Welding is available through an external fabricator

## Decision criteria

Use these criteria in the memorandum:

1. Structural stiffness and vibration behaviour
2. Ability to modify and reconfigure the stand
3. Manufacturing and assembly risk
4. Total delivered cost
5. Maintenance and repairability
6. Evidence quality and unresolved uncertainty

## Required output

Prepare a two-page research memorandum containing:

1. Executive recommendation
2. Evidence-comparison table
3. Areas of agreement and disagreement
4. Cost and risk matrix
5. Explicit limitations and missing evidence
6. Recommended next validation step

## Important constraint

Do not calculate or claim a definitive 100 kg deflection comparison unless the two frame geometries, support conditions, material properties, joint assumptions, and load locations are made comparable.

A qualified recommendation is acceptable. False precision is not.
