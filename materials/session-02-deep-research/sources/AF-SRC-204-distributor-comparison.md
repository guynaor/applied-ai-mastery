# AF-SRC-204 — Modular Frame Selection Guide

**Publisher:** BuildMotion Distribution  
**Document type:** Distributor selection guide  
**Date:** 2026-01-14

The guide compares aluminium T-slot systems with welded steel frames. It lists extrusion as faster to assemble, easier to modify, corrosion resistant, and suitable for laboratories and evolving automation equipment. It lists welded steel as lower cost for some fixed large frames and better suited to heavy permanent machinery.

The guide repeats the claim that a “properly braced 4040 frame can deflect less than 0.35 mm under an 80 kg load.” A footnote identifies ModuRail Application Note Revision 4 as the source. No independent test is described.

The guide estimates that modular construction can reduce engineering-change time by 30–50%, but provides no study method, sample size, or cost basis.

## Commercial relationship

BuildMotion distributes ModuRail extrusion and connectors. It does not fabricate welded frames.

## Useful content

The guide provides a practical checklist of factors to compare:

- Expected frequency of reconfiguration
- Joint inspection and maintenance
- Required flatness and alignment
- Corrosion protection
- Access to welding and machining
- Delivery lead time
- Need for vibration isolation or anchoring

The checklist is useful, but its quantitative claims should not be treated as independent confirmation of the ModuRail application note.
