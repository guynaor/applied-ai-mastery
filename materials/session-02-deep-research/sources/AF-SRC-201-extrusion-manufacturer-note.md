# AF-SRC-201 — 4040 Extrusion Application Note

**Publisher:** ModuRail Systems  
**Document type:** Manufacturer application note  
**Revision:** 4  
**Date:** 2025-11-18

ModuRail states that its 4040 heavy extrusion is suitable for machine guards, laboratory fixtures, light automation frames, and modular test equipment.

The note claims that bolted modular frames can be assembled rapidly, modified without cutting or welding, and repaired by replacing individual members. It also states that properly designed gussets and joining plates improve joint rigidity.

A demonstration frame measuring 900 mm × 600 mm × 700 mm carried a centrally placed 80 kg static load. The note reports less than 0.35 mm vertical movement at the centre of the upper frame.

## Conditions disclosed

- The demonstration used heavy-profile 4040 extrusion.
- Four diagonal braces were installed.
- Eight proprietary corner plates were used.
- The frame was anchored to a rigid laboratory floor.
- The reported movement includes no uncertainty or measurement-method description.
- No dynamic-excitation or long-term joint-loosening data is provided.

## Commercial information

The document estimates material and connector cost at €1,180 for the demonstration frame. Labour, shipping, anchors, and instrumentation mounting are excluded.

## Manufacturer conclusion

ModuRail recommends aluminium extrusion where frequent reconfiguration and fast assembly are more important than minimum frame mass or permanent welded rigidity.
