# AF-SRC-203 — Internal Pilot Frame Test

**Author:** Daniel Ruiz, Mechanical Engineer  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Date:** 2026-06-09  
**Status:** Preliminary; not a controlled qualification test

Engineering assembled a small 4040 extrusion frame from spare stock to support a 62 kg test fixture. The frame footprint was 700 mm × 500 mm and it was not anchored to the floor.

During a hand-applied lateral disturbance, visible motion occurred at the upper corners. Adding two diagonal braces reduced the motion substantially. After approximately 18 hours of operation near 30 Hz, two connector bolts required retightening.

A dial indicator placed near the centre of the top rail showed approximately 0.42 mm downward movement when the 62 kg fixture was installed. The indicator setup was not independently checked, and the load was offset toward one side.

## Observations

- Bracing had a large apparent effect on lateral rigidity.
- Connector preload and inspection procedure may matter in repeated dynamic service.
- The modular frame allowed the fixture position to be changed in less than one hour.
- No welded-steel comparison frame was tested.

## Author caution

These observations should guide a more controlled comparison. They should not be used to predict the behaviour of the proposed full-size frame without standardizing geometry, joints, supports, and load location.
