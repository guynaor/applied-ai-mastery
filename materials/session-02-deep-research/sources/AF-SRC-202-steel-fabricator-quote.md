# AF-SRC-202 — Welded Steel Test-Stand Proposal

**Publisher:** Kestrel Fabrication Ltd.  
**Document type:** Supplier quotation and technical proposal  
**Date:** 2026-07-22

Kestrel proposes a frame made from 50 mm × 50 mm × 3 mm mild-steel rectangular tubing with welded corner joints, two lower cross-members, and four floor-anchor plates.

The proposal states that welded steel provides high joint rigidity and is commonly used for fixed machine bases. It predicts that the proposed frame will be “substantially stiffer than an equivalent modular aluminium frame,” but no equivalent aluminium geometry or calculation is supplied.

## Included work

- Cut and welded frame
- Basic dimensional inspection
- Zinc-rich primer and topcoat
- Delivery to the AquaForge workshop
- Four levelling feet

## Excluded work

- Floor anchors
- Machined fixture plates
- Vibration isolation
- Post-installation alignment
- Design changes after approval

## Commercial information

Quoted delivered price: €1,460 excluding VAT.

Design changes after fabrication begin are billed separately. Estimated lead time is four weeks from approved drawing.

## Technical limitations

The quotation does not include finite-element analysis, modal testing, a guaranteed natural frequency, or a measured deflection limit. Welding sequence, stress relief, and final flatness tolerance are not specified.
