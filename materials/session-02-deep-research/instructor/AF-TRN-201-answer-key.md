# AF-TRN-201 — Instructor Answer Key

## Purpose

This exercise does not have one universally correct frame choice. It assesses whether the student can build a defensible recommendation from incomplete, partly dependent, and non-comparable evidence.

## Strong central finding

The supplied evidence is insufficient for a definitive stiffness or deflection winner because the aluminium and steel proposals do not use standardized geometry, joints, supports, load position, or test methods.

A strong memorandum should therefore avoid converting the available figures into a direct numerical comparison.

## Source-quality observations

### AF-SRC-201 — ModuRail application note

Useful for:

- Understanding the tested extrusion configuration
- Identifying benefits of modular assembly
- Establishing one commercial cost reference

Limitations:

- Manufacturer source
- No measurement method or uncertainty
- Static rather than dynamic test
- Different size and load from the AquaForge requirement
- Cost excludes labour, shipping, anchors, and instrumentation mounts

### AF-SRC-202 — Kestrel proposal

Useful for:

- Delivered steel price and exclusions
- Lead time
- Proposed member dimensions
- Fabrication and change-order risk

Limitations:

- Supplier assertion of superior stiffness is unsupported
- No guaranteed deflection or natural frequency
- Important fabrication tolerances are unspecified

### AF-SRC-203 — Internal pilot test

Useful for:

- Showing that bracing and connector preload matter
- Demonstrating rapid reconfiguration
- Identifying bolt-retightening as a maintenance consideration

Limitations:

- Different footprint and load
- Unanchored frame
- Offset load
- Unverified indicator setup
- No steel control frame
- Preliminary observation, not qualification evidence

### AF-SRC-204 — Distributor guide

Useful for:

- Practical comparison checklist
- Identifying commercial and operational criteria

Limitations:

- Commercial relationship with the extrusion manufacturer
- Repeats the 0.35 mm claim from AF-SRC-201
- Does not provide independent confirmation
- Change-time estimate has no method or sample basis

## Acceptable recommendation patterns

### Conditional recommendation for aluminium extrusion

This can be strong when the student argues that frequent modifications, internal assembly capability, and short change cycles are strategically important, provided that AquaForge validates dynamic performance with a representative braced and anchored prototype.

### Conditional recommendation for welded steel

This can be strong when the student prioritizes a fixed configuration and potential joint rigidity, provided that AquaForge obtains clarified flatness, welding, modal, and deflection requirements from the fabricator before purchase.

### Defer final selection pending validation

This is also acceptable when paired with a focused next step rather than vague requests for “more research.”

## Recommended next validation step

The strongest proposal is a standardized comparison using the same external dimensions, support or anchoring conditions, 100 kg load location, fixture interface, and measurement method. It should evaluate:

1. Static deflection
2. Lateral compliance
3. Natural frequencies or response over the operating range up to 35 Hz
4. Joint or fastener stability after representative endurance operation
5. Delivered cost including all exclusions

A lower-cost alternative is to request comparable calculations and guaranteed tolerances from both suppliers before constructing prototypes.

## Red flags

Reduce credit when a submission:

- Treats AF-SRC-204 as independent confirmation of AF-SRC-201
- Directly compares 0.35 mm at 80 kg with 0.42 mm at 62 kg
- Claims steel is stiffer without defining equivalent geometry and joints
- Ignores anchoring and bracing differences
- Compares €1,180 material-only cost with €1,460 delivered cost as if inclusions were equal
- Produces a precise 100 kg deflection from unsupported assumptions
- Selects an option without discussing uncertainty or validation

## Debrief questions

- Which sources are genuinely independent?
- Which disagreement is caused by different test conditions rather than contradictory physics?
- What evidence would most likely reverse the recommendation?
- Which missing cost item could materially change the decision?
- How did the AI tool handle repeated claims across sources?
