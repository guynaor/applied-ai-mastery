# AF-CAP-001 — AquaNode Mini Engineering-Validation Decision

**Requested by:** Maya Levin, Chief Executive Officer  
**Technical sponsor:** Daniel Arbel, Chief Technology Officer  
**Student role:** Lead Applied AI Engineer

## Decision to support

Recommend one of the following:

1. proceed to a bounded engineering-validation pilot;
2. revise the concept and repeat defined pre-pilot work;
3. stop the concept for now.

This is not a commercial-launch decision.

## Scenario

AquaForge has identified interest in a smaller environmental-monitoring unit. Existing course materials contain technical assumptions, preliminary costs, customer-interest signals, operational constraints, supplier observations, and an early parametric wall-bracket design. The evidence is useful but incomplete and was created for different decisions.

Your task is to reconcile that evidence into one defensible recommendation.

## Mandatory workstreams

### 1. Prompt and model strategy

Create one structured RISEN prompt for the capstone. Run it through at least two AI systems using the same evidence. Record meaningful differences and identify which claims require human verification.

### 2. Research memorandum

Produce a concise memorandum that:

- defines the decision and criteria;
- distinguishes internal evidence from external research;
- checks every important citation;
- includes the strongest case against your preferred recommendation;
- identifies evidence that would reverse the decision.

### 3. Data workbook

Build a clean workbook with:

- assumptions and data contract;
- cost model;
- pilot inventory or component register;
- risk register;
- validation-status summary;
- formulas that remain correct when unit count changes.

Do not silently replace missing values.

### 4. Leadership presentation

Create a six-slide decision deck:

1. decision requested;
2. problem and opportunity;
3. evidence and economics;
4. unknowns and risks;
5. bounded validation plan;
6. recommendation and approval gates.

Every slide must have one takeaway. Concept imagery must be labelled as concept imagery.

### 5. Pilot operations plan

Plan a fictional three-day validation sequence. Include hard constraints, travel or transition time, setup, recovery capacity, contingency rules, owners, and current-fact verification tasks.

### 6. Monitoring-agent specification

Specify a bounded agent that monitors one pilot dependency, such as supplier availability or validation-result completeness. Include trigger, source, state model, meaningful-change rule, duplicate suppression, recovery, logging, notification, failure handling, pause, and stop procedures. It must not purchase, book, or approve anything.

### 7. Parametric CAD deliverable

Extend or validate the Session 7 wall-bracket model. Provide:

- design contract;
- named parameters;
- default and changed configurations;
- fit coupon or equivalent validation plan;
- print-orientation assessment;
- known limitations.

A render is not proof of strength, fit, or certification.

## Final submission

Submit:

- executive recommendation memo;
- evidence register;
- prompt/model-comparison record;
- research memo;
- spreadsheet workbook;
- six-slide deck;
- operations plan and calendar export;
- agent specification and test log;
- OpenSCAD source and validation log;
- final decision log;
- one-page reflection on where AI helped, where it failed, and what required human judgment.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not claim regulatory compliance, field readiness, validated demand, approved budget, or structural certification.
- Mark estimates, assumptions, fictional values, and unresolved conflicts.
- Preserve source evidence and document transformations.
- Keep irreversible actions under human control.
- Recommendation strength must not exceed evidence strength.
