Shared philosophy

AI is the tool. Better judgment is the outcome.

The goal is not memorizing one product. Across all three courses, learners break down unfamiliar problems, ask better questions, compare outputs, verify facts, recognize uncertainty, and turn ideas into useful artifacts.

Skills shared by all three courses

  • Prompt design and iteration
  • Research and source checking
  • Decision frameworks
  • Data and document creation
  • Creative generation
  • Automation with safe boundaries
  • Reflection on human judgment
