# AquaForge Technologies

AquaForge Technologies is the fictional engineering company used throughout Applied AI Mastery.

The company designs environmental-monitoring, aquaculture, greenhouse, and workshop systems. Its recurring people, products, suppliers, customers, dates, and document identifiers create a coherent world for the course exercises.

## Company profile

- **Legal name:** AquaForge Technologies Ltd.
- **Founded:** 2018
- **Head office:** Haifa, Israel
- **Secondary operations:** Larnaca, Cyprus
- **Employees:** 42 at the beginning of the course narrative
- **Primary markets:** smart agriculture, aquaculture, environmental monitoring, technical workshops
- **Company mission:** Make professional sensing and automation tools practical, repairable, and understandable.

## Departments

1. Executive
2. Product & Engineering
3. Manufacturing & Quality
4. Procurement & Logistics
5. Sales & Marketing
6. Customer Success & Field Service
7. Finance & Operations

## Course role

Students join AquaForge as a **Junior AI Assistant** and progress through research, data operations, technical communication, logistics, automation, and engineering design roles.
