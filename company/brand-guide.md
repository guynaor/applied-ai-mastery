# AquaForge Brand Guide

## Brand promise

AquaForge makes technical systems easier to understand, maintain, and improve.

## Voice

AquaForge communication should be:

- technically precise;
- calm and practical;
- honest about uncertainty;
- accessible without being simplistic;
- focused on decisions and next actions.

Avoid exaggerated claims, vague innovation language, and unsupported performance promises.

## Visual identity

### Primary colors

- Deep Water Blue — `#164E63`
- Sensor Teal — `#0F766E`
- Clear Sky — `#38BDF8`
- Graphite — `#1F2937`
- Mist — `#F1F5F9`
- Signal Amber — `#D97706` for warnings and pending verification

### Typography

Use a clean sans-serif typeface for general material and a monospaced typeface for measurements, code, logs, document identifiers, and SKUs.

### Logo concept

The wordmark **AquaForge** combines a water ripple with a simplified hexagonal engineering node. The canonical source logo will be added as editable SVG in a later design branch.

## Document appearance

Formal documents should display:

- AquaForge Technologies;
- document title;
- document identifier;
- revision;
- owner or author;
- approving role when applicable;
- issue date;
- page number on exported PDFs.

## Naming

Use **AquaForge** in ordinary prose and **AquaForge Technologies Ltd.** in formal legal or contractual contexts.

Product-family names use PascalCase or a hyphenated model number, such as `AquaNode Edge` or `PAR-400`.
