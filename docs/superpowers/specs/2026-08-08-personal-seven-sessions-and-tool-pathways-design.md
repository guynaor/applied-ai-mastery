# Personal Seven-Session Course and Tool Pathways Design

## Goal

Reframe the Personal track for anyone who wants to use AI well in everyday life. Replace the twelve 20–30 minute lessons with seven integrated 45–60 minute sessions while retaining every existing activity and learning outcome. Embed practical Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini workflows in Personal, Professional, and Teacher tasks, without making paid features mandatory.

## Delivery order

1. Make the complete seven-session Personal course ready for tomorrow: integrated learner materials, instructor scripts, teaching/review notes, bilingual portal, journal, and course overview.
2. Add embedded, optional tool pathways to relevant Professional and Teacher missions in a separate follow-up after the Personal course is ready.

## Personal audience and session map

The Personal track is for people of any age or life stage who want to use AI for personal decisions, planning, communication, creativity, research, organization, and projects. It is not positioned as a young-adult or pre-career course.

| Session | Integrated outcome | Existing learning retained |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ask, Summarize, and Decide | Turn personal information into a verified decision or next step. | Lessons 1–3 |
| 2. Research, Buy, and Monitor | Research a meaningful purchase or financial question, compare evidence, and define a safe alert. | Lessons 4, 5, 8 |
| 3. Plan Real Life Together | Produce a feasible travel or event plan with shared ownership and contingencies. | Lessons 6–7 |
| 4. Build a Small Personal Tool | Specify, build, test, and maintain a simple personal web tool. | Lesson 9 |
| 5. Design and Organize a Physical Project | Turn dimensions and needs into a tested parametric design. | Lesson 10 |
| 6. Create a Trustworthy Visual Story | Create an ethical, sourced, accessible visual story. | Lesson 11 |
| 7. Build a Personal Workflow and Portfolio | Consolidate evidence, recurring workflows, personal presentation, and the final project. | Lesson 12 and the existing capstone |

Each session receives a unified learner brief and instructor flow. Existing activities remain accessible as named phases or resources within the session, not as separately completed portal lessons. Browser progress, XP, journals, and portal counts change from twelve to seven.

## Tool pathways

The course remains concept-first and vendor-flexible, but tasks include optional, concrete pathways for:

- **ChatGPT:** chat, files, research, creative artifacts, and voice/app workflows.
- **Claude Desktop:** files and desktop-connected workflows; learners define scope and inspect permissions before connecting local resources.
- **Gemini:** multimodal/personal-organization workflows and Gemini Deep Research for evidence-led research.
- **OpenClaw:** optional advanced permissions case study only. It is not required, installed, or deployed; learners define operator boundary, allowed actions, stop rules, and human approval.

Gemini Deep Research is the preferred concrete research example. It must produce a research plan, source list, evidence matrix, uncertainty log, and independent verification step; its output is not evidence by itself.

Every pathway has a no-paid-feature alternative. Learners can use a free-access tool they already have, complete the planning/verification portion without the tool, or observe a facilitator demonstration.

## Deferred cross-track embedding

Professional and Teacher receive short task-level tool-pathway sections rather than generic product lists:

- Prompting/communication tasks: optional Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini file/creation path with data-minimization reminder.
- Research tasks: Gemini Deep Research plan → sources/evidence matrix → human verification and bounded recommendation.
- Agent/automation tasks: Claude Desktop/OpenClaw permission map, allowed inputs, prohibited data/actions, audit trail, approval gate, and stop condition. No track uses autonomous grading, communications, payments, or consequential decisions.

This work is explicitly out of scope for the Personal-course implementation scheduled for tomorrow's test.

## Personal course test-ready package

Every session becomes a 45–60 minute facilitation package with unified learner materials, instructor scripts, teaching/review notes, and Hebrew counterparts. Session 1 uses the existing prompt, summary, and decision activities: AI/tool geography and privacy (10 minutes), a structured request (10), a source-grounded summary (10), a decision comparison (15), verification and journal reflection (10). It includes optional Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini variants and a facilitator note that access level must not determine success.

The English and Hebrew learner/instructor material must explain: do not paste private data; distinguish AI output from source evidence; compare at least two options/outputs; record what needs human verification; and save an evidence-based decision or next action.
