# Hebrew Teacher Grade-Band Terminology Design

## Goal

Use Israeli school terminology in every Hebrew-facing Teacher-track surface. English materials retain their existing K–12 language.

## Terminology

Hebrew text will use these three bands exclusively:

- **יסודי: א׳–ו׳**
- **חטיבה: ז׳–ט׳**
- **תיכון: י׳–י״ב**

The change removes K–12 and four-band numeric expressions such as K–2, 3–5, 6–8, and 9–12 from Hebrew Teacher portal copy, Hebrew Markdown, Hebrew journals, and Hebrew-facing validation labels. It does not alter canonical English source materials, formulas, CSV field values, code, or file names.

## Content adaptation

Where a Hebrew example currently distinguishes four developmental groups, rewrite it into the three Israeli bands without losing the instructional intent. For example, age-appropriate guided noticing belongs in יסודי, evidence-based comparison and structured discussion in חטיבה, and methodological, civic, or source critique in תיכון.

## Validation

Extend the Teacher course static contract to reject legacy K–12/four-band terms in Hebrew Teacher sources and require all three Israeli terms. Preserve the existing bilingual/localization validation suite.
