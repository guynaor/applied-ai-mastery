# Real Life Mission 4 — Research a Real Purchase

**Ability unlocked:** Smart Buyer

Choose something you were already considering buying. Do not purchase solely to complete the assignment.

## Bronze — Compare three options

Define needs and deal-breakers before searching. Compare at least three products using current sources. Check compatibility, seller, returns, warranty, and total expected cost.

## Silver — Challenge the marketing

Trace two important claims to stronger evidence. Identify repeated claims that may originate from the same source, one unresolved uncertainty, and one reason not to buy yet.

## Gold — Validate after purchase or with an owner

Where practical, compare the research with a real owner’s experience or test the product during the return window.

## Evidence to submit

- needs-first specification;
- comparison table and source links with dates;
- total-cost calculation;
- one claim you verified and one you could not;
- purchase, wait, or reject decision with reason;
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. Which factor mattered more than listed price?
2. Where did AI overstate confidence?
3. Which source was most trustworthy and why?
4. What record should you keep after buying?

## Instructor check

Reward disciplined research, including a justified decision not to purchase. Confirm that current facts were checked directly.
