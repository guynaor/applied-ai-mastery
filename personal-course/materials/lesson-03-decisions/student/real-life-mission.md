# Real Life Mission 3 — Make One Better Decision

**Ability unlocked:** Decision Architect

Choose one real, reversible, low-risk decision with two or three options. Examples include a course, phone plan, weekend activity, study method, gym, transport option, or small purchase.

## Bronze — Expose the structure

Define the decision, deadline, criteria, facts, assumptions, and unknowns. Ask AI to challenge missing criteria without recommending an option.

## Silver — Build and test the matrix

Complete a weighted decision matrix. Label every factual score with a source or observation and every unsupported score as an assumption. Change one important weight or uncertain score and record whether the preferred option changes.

## Gold — Add another perspective

Ask a trusted person to review your criteria before you decide. Record what changed and why.

## Evidence to submit

- decision statement and options;
- completed matrix;
- source and assumption notes;
- AI framing challenge;
- sensitivity test;
- decision record, reconsideration trigger, and review date;
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. Which criterion had the greatest influence?
2. Which assumption was most fragile?
3. Did the score agree with your intuition?
4. What would make you reopen the decision?

## Instructor check

The total score must remain a thinking aid rather than an automatic answer. Look for transparent values, evidence, and uncertainty.
