# Real Life Mission 7 — Organize Something With Other People

**Ability unlocked:** Collaborative Organizer

Plan a real low-stakes activity such as a movie night, meal, study session, birthday, game night, or weekend outing.

## Bronze — Coordinate clearly

Create a short poll with only the decisions that genuinely need group input. Summarize responses without inventing consensus. Build a task list with one owner and one deadline per task.

## Silver — Create the event package

Prepare:

- confirmed plan and unresolved decisions;
- invitation or announcement;
- budget or contribution plan;
- task board;
- reminder schedule;
- shopping or packing list;
- poster or visual prompt;
- cancellation or low-attendance fallback.

## Gold — Run the event

Use the plan with real participants. Afterward, collect brief feedback and record one process improvement.

## Evidence to submit

- poll and anonymized response summary;
- task board;
- invitation and visual;
- reminders and fallback;
- outcome or peer feedback if used;
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. Which decision required people rather than AI?
2. Did AI imply agreement that was not present?
3. Which task lacked a clear owner at first?
4. What would you reuse for the next event?

## Instructor check

Confirm privacy, truthful handling of group preferences, and clear ownership. A visually attractive invitation does not replace coordination evidence.
