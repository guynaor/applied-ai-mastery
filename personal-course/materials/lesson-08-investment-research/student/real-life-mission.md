# Real Life Mission 8 — Research an Investment Without Predicting

**Ability unlocked:** Evidence-Balanced Researcher

Choose one public company, ETF, industry, or broad investment idea you genuinely want to understand. This is a research exercise, not personalized financial advice.

## Bronze — Build a balanced brief

Write a neutral research question. Gather at least one primary source and one credible independent source. Record publication dates and distinguish facts, interpretations, and unknowns.

## Silver — Argue both sides

Create a short brief containing:

- what the investment is
- reasons someone might consider it
- reasons someone might avoid it
- key risks and uncertainties
- important metrics or events to monitor
- claims that remain unverified
- conditions that would justify revisiting the research.

Do not ask AI for a target price, guaranteed return, or prediction.

## Gold — Defend the opposite view

Ask another person or AI to challenge your strongest conclusion using evidence. Revise the brief where the challenge is valid.

## Evidence to submit

- research question
- dated source list
- balanced brief
- one weak or stale claim you rejected
- revision after challenge
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. Which source most changed your understanding?
2. Where did AI sound more certain than the evidence allowed?
3. What remains unknowable?
4. What would you monitor rather than predict?

## Instructor check

The submission must remain balanced and non-advisory. Reward uncertainty handling and opposing evidence, not a bullish or bearish conclusion.
