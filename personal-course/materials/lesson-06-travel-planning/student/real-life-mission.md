# Real Life Mission 6 — Plan a Realistic Day or Trip

**Ability unlocked:** Constraint-Based Planner

Plan a real weekend, day trip, visit, or future journey. A local day is sufficient; no booking or spending is required.

## Bronze — Build a feasible plan

Record hard constraints, preferences, budget, opening windows, travel origins, travel times, buffers, and one fallback.

## Silver — Stress-test and organize

Test both:

1. arrival is delayed by 90 minutes
2. one planned activity becomes unavailable.

Update the itinerary, then create calendar entries, a shared-note structure, booking owners, and staged recheck dates.

## Gold — Use or peer-test the plan

Follow part of the itinerary or ask another person to audit it for hidden travel time, unrealistic pacing, or missing costs.

## Evidence to submit

- constraint register
- timed itinerary and budget
- source links and verification dates
- results of both disruption tests
- fallback plan and recheck schedule
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. Which constraint most shaped the plan?
2. What did AI underestimate or omit?
3. Which facts will become stale first?
4. What did you remove to make the plan realistic?

## Instructor check

Look for feasibility, buffers, current-source checking, and graceful response to change—not the maximum number of activities.
