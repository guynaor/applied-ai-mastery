# Real Life Mission 2 — Turn Information into Action

**Ability unlocked:** Finding the Signal

Choose one real source you genuinely need to understand: a lecture, long email, article, meeting note, podcast transcript, policy, or instructions.

## Bronze — Make a verified summary

Create a purpose-specific summary, then compare every important claim against the source. Correct unsupported additions and mark anything uncertain.

## Silver — Build an action package

Produce:

- a short summary
- key facts and open questions
- tasks with owners and dates where supported
- calendar items
- a follow-up message or question list.

Do not invent deadlines or commitments.

## Gold — Compare formats

Create two versions for different audiences or purposes, such as a one-minute overview and detailed study notes. Ask another person which version is more useful and why.

## Evidence to submit

- source title or safe excerpt/link
- prompt used
- first summary and corrected summary
- verification notes
- extracted actions or calendar items
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. What important detail was easiest to lose?
2. Did AI add anything not supported by the source?
3. Which format matched your purpose best?
4. What information still required human follow-up?

## Instructor check

The student must demonstrate source traceability. A polished summary without checking is incomplete.
