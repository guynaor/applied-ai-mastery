# Real Life Mission 11 — Tell a Real Story Responsibly

**Ability unlocked:** Visual Storyteller

Choose a real event, trip, celebration, hobby, or week in your life. Create a short visual recap for a defined audience. Use only media you own or are permitted to use.

## Bronze — Build a clear story

Organize source files, choose 4–6 story beats, and create a storyboard with captions. Separate original, edited, generated, and exported assets.

## Silver — Add one responsible generated asset

Create one support image, title card, background, transition concept, or caption treatment with AI. Do not fabricate a real event or place a real person into something they did not do. Label generated or substantially altered media where the audience could be misled.

Complete checks for:

- consent
- privacy and location exposure
- copyright or licence
- factual captions
- accessibility, including readable text and useful alt text or subtitles
- export quality and format.

## Gold — Share and revise

Show the draft to one intended viewer. Ask what was clear, confusing, too long, or potentially misleading. Revise one part.

## Evidence to submit

- storyboard
- prompt for the generated support asset
- source and permission notes
- final export or private link
- before/after revision note
- completed journal entry.

## Reflection

1. What did you leave out and why?
2. Could viewers distinguish real from generated material?
3. Whose consent mattered?
4. Which file-organization habit saved time?

## Instructor check

Assess narrative clarity, provenance, consent, and authenticity—not production value alone.
