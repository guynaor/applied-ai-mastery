# Session 1 facilitator guide — Decide What to Do Next

## Preparation and timing

Prepare a public event/class page and a non-sensitive choice between two options. Teach: privacy/tool framing 5 minutes; frame request 10; purposeful summary 10; compare 15; verification and reflection 15 (55 total).

## Default demonstration

Use Claude with: **“I am choosing between a Saturday gardening class and a museum talk. I have 3 hours, a 30-unit budget, and want a social activity. From this public page, extract dates, costs, commitments, unknowns, and claims I must verify. Then make a two-option comparison; do not choose for me.”** Expected intermediate artifact: a facts/preferences/unknowns table.

## Equivalent tool routes

**Claude:** use the default request. **ChatGPT:** paste the same request and public excerpt. **Gemini:** use the same request and source. Learners may choose one route, paper, or observation.

## Visible verification and teaching notes

Open the page and visibly check date and cost; change the table if needed. Stress that a tidy answer is not a source. Common misconceptions: “more detail means more truth” and “AI made the decision.” No-paid/no-tool alternative: highlight the source and fill the comparison table by hand.

## Review prompts

Which fact could change your decision? What is preference rather than evidence? What will you do next, and when will you revisit it?
