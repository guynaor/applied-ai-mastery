Home Lab: quiet dishwashers compared

  • We measured the AS-640 in an open 9 m² kitchen and got 46 dB, not 42.
  • The NW-2 measured 47 dB for us — so the real gap between the two models is smaller than the sales pages suggest.
  • Installing the AS-640 requires an approved technician to keep the warranty valid. The market rate is around 250.
  • Both models sometimes need a longer inlet hose, about 80, if the tap is far from the opening.
