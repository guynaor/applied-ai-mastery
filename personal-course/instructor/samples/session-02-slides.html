03 · The boundaryMinutes 15–25

Four boxes, and the one everyone skips

The need

A quiet dishwasher that fits the 60 cm gap.

Budget and deadline

Up to 3,000, installed before the holidays.

Must have

60 cm wide, 44 dB or less, three-year warranty.

Rules it out

No local service. Needs the pipes moved.

What this will not decide

Whether a new machine is needed at all, or whether repairing the old one is better.

04 · The requestMinute 25

Ask for a plan, not an answer

The full request
Plan research for buying a quiet 60 cm dishwasher under 3,000. Do not recommend a model. Set out which kinds of sources establish total cost, warranty, return policy and durability, and which question each source suits. Add one opposing viewpoint and a list of what is still unknown.

Two phrases do all the work: plan, not “recommend” — and what is still unknown.

10 · The loopMinutes 60–80

What it does, over and over

It never buys. It never logs in. It tells you, and you decide.

11 · The contractMinutes 60–80

Written down before it runs

The full contract

Every six hours, read only these two public pages. Alert only if the total cost drops to 3,000 or below, if availability changes, or if a warranty or return term changes. Save the last value and the source date. Do not repeat an identical alert for 48 hours. If a source fails to load twice, send one needs review notice and pause. Do not buy, do not log in, do not send messages, do not touch private data, do not infer a recommendation.

All addresses sit under applied-ai-mastery.web.app — the two pages: /s2/source-1 (KitchenPlus), /s2/source-2 (DirectElectric). Live readings for the demo: /s2/snapshot-a, /s2/snapshot-b, /s2/snapshot-c.
