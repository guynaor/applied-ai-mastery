01 · OpeningMinutes 10–15

What are you organising right now that needs other people to happen?

We are not learning a planning method today. We are doing the work — quantities, a display, a timeline, and who actually owns what.

02 · The three questionsMinutes 10–15

Ask these after every single output

What did it assume?

Settled without asking you. Usually a number.

What did it invent?

Stated like a fact. Reads exactly like the real ones.

Who owns it?

A named person, or it does not happen.

These three are the whole session. Everything after this is practising them on real jobs.

03 · QuantitiesMinutes 15–30

Ask for the workings, not the number

The full request
We are 40 people at a four-hour evening event, including 3 vegetarians and 4 children. The food budget is fixed. Give quantities for food and drinks — and list every assumption you made to get there, as a separate list.

That last sentence is the whole difference. Without it you get numbers and no idea where they came from.

04 · What it assumedMinutes 15–30

It will settle all of these, confidently

1½ portions each
the gap between 40 and 60 covers is real money
2 drinks an hour
doubles the drinks order
Children eat like adults
or half. It did not ask
Everyone stays to the end
decides how much food is thrown away

05 · The displayMinutes 30–50

Something that has to run on the night

The full request
Build one self-contained web page that shows images on a loop: each image for 8 seconds, a caption beneath, no controls on screen, and it must keep running untouched for four hours. Tell me what it does when an image fails to load.

06 · Two checksMinutes 30–50

Before you trust it on the night

Only two images
does the loop still work, or does it flicker?
A very long caption
does the layout break, or cover the picture?

Test the edges before the night, not on it. Both take under a minute.

07 · TimingMinutes 50–65

Which of these can actually change?

Three have a source and a date. One has neither.

08 · OwnershipMinutes 65–80

“Someone should” is not a task

What the tool writes

“Invitations need sending”
“Confirm numbers”
“Sort out music”

What actually happens

Aunt, by 1 October
Partner, by 28 October
My brother, plus a backup playlist

And the part it cannot know: who is genuinely free, and who has already taken on too much.

09 · What breaksMinutes 80–90

One card, three things on it

  1. What is most likely to break.
  2. What we do instead, decided now.
  3. Who makes that call on the night.

“We’ll see what happens” postpones the decision to the moment it costs most.

10 · Exit checkMinutes 80–90

Finish the sentence

It saved me . Its assumption that surprised me most was . The thing I will not hand over is .

And the question worth sitting with

Which invented detail would you have believed if you had been in a hurry?
