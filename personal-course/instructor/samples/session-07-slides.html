01 · The app in real useMinutes 0–30

Did you actually use it — or did it quietly stop?

The version that survives contact with your real week is worth more than the one that demos well.

If you stopped using it, that is a finding about the problem, not a failure of the build.

02 · What to askMinutes 0–30

Three questions, and none of them are “does it work”

Used it when
which day, doing what — or never
Broke on
the first thing real life did to it
Stopped because
the most useful answer in the room

Do not fix anyone’s app here. Thirty minutes disappears into one person’s bug — note it, offer to look after, move on.

03 · The capstoneMinutes 30–55

Not new work. Every session, once, on one subject.

04 · The subject gateMinutes 30–55

Nothing starts until the subject is confirmed

The test, out loud: does each of the five arise from this subject naturally?

If you would have to invent a reason to design an object, or the “app” is a spreadsheet with a different name, it is the wrong subject.

05 · The one that failsMinutes 30–55

“Learning guitar”

Which two of the five fail, and why
Object
nothing to design — you would invent a reason for a plectrum holder
App
a practice log is a spreadsheet wearing a different name
Narrowed
“setting up a practice corner in a shared flat” — now all five are real

06 · What you leave withMinutes 55–60

A subject, and a date you set yourself

Nothing else anchors the capstone. There is no next class.

Anything not confirmed in the room gets confirmed in the group within a day — sitting on an unconfirmed subject is how people stall.
