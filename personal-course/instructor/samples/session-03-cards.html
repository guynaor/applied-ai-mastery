Disruption cards — Session 3

Do not share with learners before minute 50. A plan built in the knowledge of what will break is not really being tested. This page is separate from the planning sources precisely so an AI tool can be given the sources without being handed the cards.

Pick one card, or drop this link into the chat when the time comes and let each learner choose.

Card A — the weather

Heavy rain forecast between 17:00 and 19:00 on the day. The yard parking floods in rain like that.

Card B — no reply

Twelve days to go. Eight guests still have not confirmed, and the caterer closes numbers in two days.

Card C — a key person drops out

Two days before, whoever is responsible for music says they are ill and cannot come.

Card D — the venue changes the terms

Three weeks before, the hall says street works will block access to the yard that evening.

Card E — the numbers moved

Four days before, six more guests confirm. The caterer has already closed numbers.

Card F — the tool got it wrong

It turns out the quantities the AI worked out assumed two portions per person rather than one, so the order is double what is needed.

What to do with the card

Not “we'll see what happens”. A fallback needs three things:

What happens insteadWho triggers itBy when it must be decided

The conflicts already sitting in the sources

These live in the planning sources themselves and should surface while the plan is being arranged — not here.

  • The hall is available until 22:00 with full vacation by 22:30, but a typical plan stretches to 23:00.
  • Route 47 departs last at 22:40, so anyone travelling by bus has to leave before the event ends.
  • The caterer closes numbers 10 days before, and printed invitations take 7 working days after design approval.
  • A fourth, for anyone hunting: the caterer clears equipment within an hour of the end, and the hall requires full vacation by 22:30.
