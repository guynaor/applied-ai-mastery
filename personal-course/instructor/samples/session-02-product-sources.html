Source 3 — manufacturer page

AquaSilent — official warranty terms

  • Manufacturer warranty: two years on parts and labour.
  • A third year is granted only if the product is registered on the manufacturer's site within 30 days of purchase.
  • The warranty does not apply if installation was not carried out by a manufacturer-approved technician.
  • The stated noise level is measured under laboratory conditions, on the eco programme, in a closed kitchen.

Last updated: 3 February

Source 4 — independent review

Home Lab: quiet dishwashers compared

  • We measured the AS-640 in an open 9 m² kitchen and got 46 dB, not 42.
  • The NW-2 measured 47 dB for us — so the real gap between the two models is smaller than the sales pages suggest.
  • Installing the AS-640 requires an approved technician to keep the warranty valid. The market rate is around 250.
  • Both models sometimes need a longer inlet hose, about 80, if the tap is far from the opening.

Published: 28 April
