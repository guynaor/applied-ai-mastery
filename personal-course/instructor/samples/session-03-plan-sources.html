Sample planning sources — Session 3

A stable local source set for the Session 3 demonstration. These are not real booking pages, they contain no payment links, and they hold no personal details. Use them so the session works without a network, and so the times and prices do not change mid-explanation.

The scenario: a 40-person family event on 7 November. Three real conflicts are planted between the sources — they are meant to surface while the plan is being built, not before.

Source 1 — venue page

Community House — small hall

  • Hall available: 08:00–22:00. Full vacation by 22:30.
  • Evening rate: 6,400, tables and chairs included.
  • Cleaning: 400, mandatory.
  • Cancellation: full refund up to 30 days before. After that 50%. Under 14 days, no refund.
  • Outside catering: permitted, with at least one week's notice.
  • Parking: 12 spaces in the yard.

Last updated: 3 September

Source 2 — caterer page

Field Kitchen — event quote

  • Per head: 95. Minimum 30 covers.
  • Arrival to set up: 90 minutes before the event starts.
  • Equipment collected: within one hour after the event ends.
  • Final numbers confirmed: 10 days before the event. After that, billed at the number given.
  • No menu changes in the final week.

Last updated: 21 August

Disruption cards

Pick one card for the “find where the plan breaks” step. Do not hand them out in advance.

Card A

Weather

Heavy rain forecast between 17:00 and 19:00 on the day. The yard parking floods in rain like that.

Card B

No reply

Twelve days to go. Eight guests still have not confirmed, and the caterer closes numbers in two days.

Card C

A key person drops out

Two days before, whoever is responsible for music says they are ill and cannot come.

Card D

The venue changes the terms

Three weeks before, the hall says street works will block access to the yard that evening.
