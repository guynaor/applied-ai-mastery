10 · “Sturdier”Minutes 56–76

“Sturdier” is not a dimension

A feeling

“Make it sturdier”

A dimension

“Leg section 8×8 cm,

Feelings get answers that look good. Dimensions get answers you can check.

11 · What breaksMinutes 56–76

The span between legs

One change, and two other things quietly became wrong.

Say the number out loud before you change it.

12 · File to objectMinutes 76–86

Physics did not read the file

Tolerance

Geometry
A 0.4 mm wall, an overhang, a part floating in mid-air
Orientation
The same object, rotated — snaps or holds

The model was perfect on screen. This is where “virtual” ends.
