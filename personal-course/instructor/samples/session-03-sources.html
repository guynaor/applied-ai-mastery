Planning sources — Session 3

The facts for the sample scenario: a 40-person family event on 7 November, on a midweek evening. These are not real booking pages and contain no personal details.

If your tool cannot open links, copy from here and paste it into the chat. If it can, just give it this page's address.

Real conflicts are planted between these sources. They are meant to surface while the plan is being arranged, not before.

Source 1 — the venue

Community House — small hall

  • Hall available: 08:00–22:00. Full vacation by 22:30.
  • Evening rate: 6,400, tables and chairs included.
  • Cleaning: 400, mandatory.
  • Cancellation: full refund up to 30 days before. After that 50%. Under 14 days, no refund.
  • Outside catering: permitted, with at least one week's notice.
  • Parking: 12 spaces in the yard.

Last updated: 3 September
