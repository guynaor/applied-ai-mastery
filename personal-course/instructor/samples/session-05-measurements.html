Clearances to leave

  • A finger has to get in to lift something out: at least 1.5 cm around each compartment.
  • The organiser must not touch the runner above: leave 0.5 cm.
  • If 3D printing or cutting from cardboard, the wall thickness itself eats into the internal dimension.

The adjustable parameter

The number of cable compartments. Change it from 5 to 7 and write down in advance what should happen to the width of each one — then check whether that is what happened.

The cardboard test

Cut a 36 x 42 cm rectangle, pencil the compartments onto it, put it in the drawer, and try taking things out and putting them back as you would on an ordinary day.
