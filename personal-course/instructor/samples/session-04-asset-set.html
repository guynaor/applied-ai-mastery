Sample asset set — Session 4

One story: “volunteering day at the community garden”. Six assets, each with its source, the consents attached to it, and the claim it is meant to support.

There are deliberately no real photographs here. What this session examines is what is written beside the image, not how it looks. Four assets are sound and two are not — let the class find them before opening the answers.

Accessibility check

The checkWhat to do
Alt textA sentence per image describing what is visible, not “a photo of the garden”
ContrastWhite text over a bright image almost always fails
Not colour aloneIf “before” and “after” are marked only by colour, add a word too
Read it aloudRead the story from the alt text alone. Does it still make sense?
