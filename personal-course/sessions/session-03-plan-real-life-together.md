# Session 3 — Make a Shared Plan Work

**Time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Outcome:** Turn one shared trip, event, or commitment into a feasible plan with ownership and a fallback.

## Scenario

You are coordinating a small trip, an event, or another shared commitment. Different people have availability, budget, access needs, and preferences. The goal is not a perfect-looking itinerary; it is a plan people can actually use. Keep personal contacts, bookings, and precise locations out of an AI chat.

## Sequence

1. **Name the real constraints (10 minutes).** List fixed dates, budget, accessibility needs, commitments, and decision owner. Separate these from preferences.
2. **Collect changing facts (10 minutes).** Find and date the essential details—travel time, opening hours, availability, costs, booking rules, or event requirements. Keep links beside each fact.
3. **Build one workable plan (15 minutes).** Ask Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini to organise only your supplied facts: fixed commitments first, then tasks, travel, rest, buffers, owners, and deadlines. Draft a privacy-minimising invitation or poll only if your scenario needs one.
4. **Break it safely (10 minutes).** Test one realistic disruption: late arrival, weather, a sold-out option, or a missing response. Add a fallback, assign who acts, and mark what must be rechecked before booking or sending.

## Integrated artifact

Create a shareable plan with sourced and dated facts, budget, ownership, calendar actions, buffers, contingency, and a recheck list. The people involved make the choices.
