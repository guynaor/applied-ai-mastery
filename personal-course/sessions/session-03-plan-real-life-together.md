# Session 3 — Plan Real Life Together

**Time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Outcome:** Produce a feasible trip or event plan with shared ownership, buffers, and verified changing facts.

## Integrated studio flow

1. **Choose and frame — 8 minutes.** Plan either a one-to-three-day trip or a small event. Separate hard constraints (date, budget, accessibility, commitments) from preferences; name a decision owner.
2. **Gather evidence — 12 minutes.** Record sources and dates for travel time, opening hours, availability, costs, booking needs, and exclusions. Do not upload private contact details, bookings, or location-sensitive information.
3. **Draft together — 15 minutes.** Ask AI to organize only supplied facts into a feasible itinerary or event plan: fixed commitments first, then travel, rest, buffers, tasks, deadlines, and fallbacks. For an event, create a short privacy-minimizing poll and one invitation draft.
4. **Stress-test and share — 10–15 minutes.** Test a late arrival, sold-out option, weather change, or missing response. Assign owners and review dates. Verify changing facts near booking or sending invitations.

## Optional tool and free-access paths

Use Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini to turn your non-sensitive table into a plan, poll, task list, or invitation. Compare an AI proposal with your constraints before sharing. Without an accessible tool, complete the workbook by hand or observe a facilitator; quality is measured by feasibility and verification, not subscription.

## Integrated artifact and completion check

Submit a trip itinerary or event plan with sources/dates, budget, owners, deadlines, buffers, fallback, and a recheck list. Confirm that the group—not AI—made choices, and that changing details will be verified before action.
