# My AI Learning Journal

Use this workbook throughout all 7 integrated sessions. Each session has one tab: a sequence of work that leads to one useful, checked result.

## A simple weekly rhythm

- Start with a real situation, a constraint, and a useful outcome.
- Save the prompt, workflow, or artifact that actually helped.
- Verify the part that matters before relying on it; record the evidence you saved.
- Reflect on your own judgment and name one improvement for next time.
