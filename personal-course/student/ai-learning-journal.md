# My AI Learning Journal

Use this journal throughout all 12 lessons. Keep it in one document, notebook, or folder so your best prompts, mistakes, checks, and projects stay together.

## How to use it

After every lesson and Real Life Mission, add one dated entry. Do not write what you think the instructor wants to hear. Record what actually happened.

## Entry template

### Lesson and date

- Lesson:
- Date:
- Real-life task:

### This week’s biggest AI win

What became easier, clearer, faster, or more creative?

### Prompt worth saving

Copy the exact prompt. Remove personal or sensitive information first.

### What made the prompt work?

Record the useful context, constraints, examples, requested format, or verification instruction.

### This week’s most useful AI mistake

What was wrong, unsupported, misleading, incomplete, or badly framed?

### How I checked it

Record the source, calculation, comparison, test, or person you used.

### Something I learned about myself

What preference, assumption, habit, strength, or blind spot did the exercise reveal?

### What I would do differently next time

Name one concrete change.

### One future use or automation idea

What repeated task could benefit from this skill?

### Evidence saved

List file names, links, screenshots, prompts, or outputs included in your submission.

## Prompt library

Keep a separate section containing only prompts you would genuinely reuse. For each prompt, add:

- purpose;
- exact prompt;
- when not to use it;
- what must be verified;
- date last improved.

## End-of-course reflection

After Lesson 12, review the full journal and answer:

1. Which three AI abilities improved most?
2. Which mistake pattern appeared repeatedly?
3. Which verification method became a habit?
4. Which artifact best demonstrates your ability?
5. What will you continue using after the course?
6. What personal information should never be placed into an AI system?

Your journal is private by default. Submit only the entries or extracts agreed with your instructor.
