# Applied AI Mastery — Everyday Life

## Audience

Young adults before university, during university, or shortly after graduation who have limited professional experience and want to use AI confidently in everyday life.

## Course goal

Teach practical AI-assisted problem solving for personal decisions, planning, communication, creativity, career preparation, purchasing, financial research, and small personal projects.

The course does not aim to make students dependent on AI. It teaches them to decide when AI is useful, provide the right context, compare options, verify important claims, protect private information, and retain responsibility for consequential decisions.

## Lesson format

Every lesson is designed for **20–30 minutes**:

1. **2–3 minutes — Hook:** a familiar problem or decision.
2. **4–6 minutes — Concept:** one durable AI skill in plain language.
3. **10–15 minutes — Guided task:** create or improve a real artifact.
4. **3–5 minutes — Check:** verify, compare, or stress-test the result.
5. **1–2 minutes — Takeaway:** one reusable rule and a small optional extension.

Each lesson must be independently useful while also building toward a personal capstone.

## Proposed syllabus

| Lesson | Title | Everyday outcome | Core AI skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask Better, Get Better | Turn a vague request into a useful result | Context, constraints, examples, iteration |
| 2 | Summarize Without Losing What Matters | Convert a long message, article, or document into decisions and actions | Structured summarization, missing-information checks |
| 3 | Build an Honest CV | Create a tailored CV and short application message without exaggeration | Evidence-based writing, audience adaptation |
| 4 | Make a Difficult Decision | Compare options using explicit criteria instead of intuition alone | Decision matrices, assumptions, sensitivity |
| 5 | Buy Smarter Online | Research products, compare real costs, and detect misleading claims | Product research, source quality, total-cost comparison |
| 6 | Create a Deal Alert | Specify a safe monitor for price or availability changes | Triggers, thresholds, duplicate suppression, stop rules |
| 7 | Plan a Realistic Trip | Build a feasible itinerary with budget, travel time, buffers, and fallbacks | Constraint-based planning, verification timing |
| 8 | Plan a Party With Friends | Coordinate preferences, budget, tasks, invitations, and a poster | Collaborative planning, creative generation |
| 9 | Research an Investment Without Pretending to Predict | Build a balanced research brief and identify risks and unknowns | Evidence synthesis, opposing views, uncertainty |
| 10 | Build a Small Poll App | Create a simple shareable app for choosing a destination, date, menu, or activity | Requirements, rapid prototyping, testing |
| 11 | Design a Simple Piece of Furniture | Turn room dimensions and needs into a parametric CAD concept | Design contracts, parameters, validation boundaries |
| 12 | Tell the Story | Create an image/video trip or event summary suitable for sharing | Storyboarding, image/video prompting, consent and authenticity |
| Final | Personal Life Project | Combine several skills to solve one genuine personal need | Integration, verification, reflection |

## Suggested personal capstone

The student chooses one real project, such as:

- planning a trip with friends;
- organizing a graduation party;
- preparing for a job application cycle;
- researching and purchasing an expensive product;
- designing a small room or furniture improvement;
- building a simple poll or coordination app.

The capstone should include:

- a clear goal and constraints;
- a prompt or workflow plan;
- at least two compared options or outputs;
- evidence and assumptions;
- one created artifact;
- a verification checklist;
- a short reflection on what AI helped with and what still required human judgment.

## Safety and judgment boundaries

- Investment lessons teach research, not personalized financial advice or predictions.
- Purchasing lessons must distinguish advertised price from total cost, returns, warranty, and seller reliability.
- Travel information that can change must be verified near booking and departure.
- CV content must remain truthful and supported by the student's experience.
- Images and videos involving real people require consent and should not misrepresent events.
- Personal, financial, identity, health, and account information should not be pasted into AI tools without understanding privacy implications.
