# My Applied AI Professional Journal

Use this journal across all seven AquaForge missions and the AquaNode Mini capstone. It should show how your prompts, evidence, checks, artifacts, and professional judgment improve over the course.

## Before you write

- Make your own copy of this template before adding entries
- Remove private, sensitive, confidential, or credential information
- Reference evidence by file name, source ID, row, cell, or section where possible
- Separate verified facts, assumptions, unknowns, and recommendations
- Keep consequential decisions under human review and approval

## Mission entry template

Create one entry after every mission.

- Mission:

### Work problem and required decision

What did AquaForge need, and what decision or deliverable did your work support?

### Evidence used

List the sources you used. Identify any conflicting, incomplete, stale, or low-confidence evidence.

### Prompt or workflow worth reusing

Record the exact prompt, formula, checklist, or sequence. Explain the context and constraints that made it useful.

### Where AI failed or remained uncertain

What did the AI invent, omit, oversimplify, miscalculate, misread, or leave unresolved?

### Verification performed and result

Record the source trace, calculation, spreadsheet check, test case, comparison, rendering check, or review used for verification. State the result.

### Human decision or approval point

What required human judgment? Who would need to approve publication, purchasing, messaging, automation, fabrication, or another real-world action?

### Artifact and evidence saved

List the deliverable files, evidence records, decision records, screenshots, or test results you retained.

### Transfer to real work

Where could this method help in a real organization? What would need to change before using it outside the simulation?

### Capstone connection

Which fact, method, artifact, risk, or decision from this mission may affect AquaNode Mini?

## Mission-specific reflection prompts

### Mission 1 - Advanced Prompting and Model Selection

Which model or interface was most suitable for each task, and which RISEN prompt change improved the output most?

### Mission 2 - Deep Research and Information Synthesis

Which evidence changed your recommendation, and how did you prevent a weaker source from receiving too much weight?

### Mission 3 - Spreadsheet Engineering

Which data-quality defect was hardest to detect, and how did your process preserve the original evidence while correcting the working data?

### Mission 4 - Presentations and Visual Artifacts

Which claim-to-source link made the leadership recommendation more credible, and what did you remove to keep the message bounded?

### Mission 5 - Constraint-Based Operations Planning

Which hard constraint or contingency most affected feasibility, and how did you verify travel time, buffers, and calendar output?

### Mission 6 - Bounded Agent Workflows

Which state, recovery, duplicate-suppression, or human-control rule prevented the most serious failure?

### Mission 7 - Parametric CAD

Which measured interface or parameter had the greatest design effect, and which digital result still requires physical verification?

### Capstone - AquaNode Mini

Which evidence dependency created the largest update across deliverables, and why is your final recommendation proceed, revise, or stop for now?

## Reusable professional workflow library

For every workflow worth keeping, record:

- purpose and intended user
- required inputs and source quality
- exact prompt, formula, checklist, or sequence
- expected output and acceptance criteria
- known failure modes
- required verification
- human approval boundary
- date last improved

## End-of-course reflection

1. Which professional AI capability improved most?
2. Which failure pattern now changes how you prompt or verify?
3. Which artifact best demonstrates traceability from evidence to decision?
4. Where did human judgment override or constrain an AI suggestion?
5. Which workflow is ready to adapt to real work, and what safeguards must be added first?
6. Which capability still needs deliberate practice?

This journal may mention simulated company information, but it should never contain real private, sensitive, confidential, or credential information.
