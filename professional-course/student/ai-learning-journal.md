# My Applied AI Professional Journal

Use this workbook across all seven AquaForge missions and the AquaNode Mini capstone. It should show how your prompts, evidence, checks, artifacts, and professional judgment improve.

## Before you write

1. Choose **File -> Make a copy** and work only in your own copy
2. Remove real private, sensitive, confidential, or credential information
3. Reference evidence by file name, source ID, row, cell, or section
4. Separate verified facts, assumptions, unknowns, and recommendations
5. Keep consequential actions under human review and approval

## Mission workflow

- Complete the mission-specific workbook tab
- Save one reusable prompt, formula, checklist, or workflow
- Perform the required verification before recommending action
- Record the evidence and artifact files you retained
- Add a short reflection and a transfer-to-work boundary

## Verification and evidence

Use source traces, calculations, spreadsheet checks, test cases, comparisons, render checks, and human review. Save the evidence that supports each important decision.

## Reflection and human judgment

Which decision, approval, or professional responsibility could not be delegated to AI?

## What I will change next time

Record one improvement to your process before the next mission.
