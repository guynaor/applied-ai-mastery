# Teacher Track Capstone — Water in Our Shared World

This capstone brings the seven Teacher-track missions into one teacher-reviewed, K–12-ready unit-design decision. The scenario, learner work, and gradebook patterns are fictional; do not add real learner names, grades, work, family contact details, or protected information.

## Mission

Act as the teacher responsible for adapting the fictional **Water in Our Shared World** unit for a chosen grade band. Build a coherent package that turns evidence and constraints into teachable choices while keeping professional judgment with the teacher.

## Required package

- `student/EDU-CAP-001-unit-plan-brief.md` — assignment and non-negotiable boundaries
- `student/EDU-CAP-002-evidence-register.csv` — source, claim, limitation, and verification record
- `student/EDU-CAP-003-deliverable-register.csv` — artifact dependency and review checklist
- `student/EDU-CAP-004-decision-log.csv` — assumptions, revisions, ownership, and unresolved questions
- `instructor/` — facilitation, assessment, and rubric materials

The final package contains a lesson sequence, four-band differentiation, evidence choices, an assessment rubric, a fictional gradebook-pattern analysis, a family communication draft, a six-slide lesson deck, and reflection. An optional extension may be a local resource hub **or** a bounded planning-digest design. It is never a real deployment.

## Completion standard

Every consequential instructional choice must be traceable to a source, a stated constraint, or a clearly labelled teacher judgment. Accessible materials, privacy boundaries, and uncertainty are part of quality—not optional polish.
