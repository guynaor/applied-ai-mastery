# EDU-TRN-500 — שלב אחר שלב שיעור Script

**Time:** 80 minutes. **Outcome:** participants turn אילוצים into realistic 10-day יחידת לימוד ו־ locate their own החלטה points.

1. **0–10 min — Launch.** Say: “אילוצים are not defects in plan; they are conditions that make it teachable.” Read the יחידת לימוד תקציר.
2. **10–22 min — Decode.** In pairs, annotate the אילוצים CSV. Ask them to circle 35-minute day, shared חומרים, accommodations, הערכה dates, ו־ contingencies.
3. **22–48 min — Sequence.** Participants complete Days 2–10. Require one formative בדיקה before Day 6 ו־ a contingency עבור outdoor work or technology failure.
4. **48–63 min — Differentiate.** Model difference between lowering task ו־ changing access. Participants complete the התאמה דיפרנציאלית plan.
5. **63–73 min — Communicate.** Draft the משפחה calendar. Ask: “Does this invite partnership without collecting household נתונים or assigning families teaching work?”
6. **73–80 min — בדיקה.** Partners audit checkpoints עבור Days 3, 6, ו־ 9. Exit ticket: name one החלטה only a מורה can make after reviewing learner ראיות.
