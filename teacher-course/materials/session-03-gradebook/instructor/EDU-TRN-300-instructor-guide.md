# EDU-TRN-300 — Instructor Guide

The exercise is spreadsheet literacy and responsible interpretation, not predictive analytics. Require preservation of raw data, documented cleaning, and an explicit missing-data check. Suitable observations: Harbor and Atlas have lower displayed scores with a `review` flag; Willow has a missing river-map entry; River’s `NA` cannot be interpreted as a score. None justifies a learner-level action.

Invite grade-band appropriate follow-ups: K–2 may re-offer a picture-supported demonstration; 3–5 may check map directions; 6–8 may revisit evidence structure; 9–12 may ask learners to inspect rubric evidence. These are teacher-designed options after review, never automated prescriptions.
