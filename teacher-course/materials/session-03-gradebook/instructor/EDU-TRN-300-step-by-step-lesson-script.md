# EDU-TRN-300 — Step-by-Step Lesson Script

## 0–10 minutes: establish the data boundary

State: “These aliases and scores are fictional. In real work, do not paste student data into an AI tool.” Read the prohibition on autonomous grading, placement, behavior, and intervention decisions aloud.

## 10–25 minutes: clean with formulas

Participants preserve raw cells, use `=IFERROR(VALUE(cell),"")`, and mark missingness. Demonstrate why blank/NA is not zero.

## 25–40 minutes: inspect, do not decide

Participants produce a question-based brief and review template. Challenge causal language and deficit labels.

## Exit check

Ask: “What original evidence must a teacher review before acting?”
