# EDU-COM-401 — Presentation Brief

**Scenario:** Your fictional school is beginning a water inquiry. Build a six-slide deck titled **Local Water, Shared Responsibility** for one grade band. It introduces how water reaches the community, why careful use matters, and one evidence-based action learners can discuss.

## Required slide structure

1. **Welcome/question:** “Where does our water go?” Use one uncluttered, locally appropriate visual.
2. **System:** Explain a verified local or generic water-cycle/water-system idea; label what is known and what needs local checking.
3. **Evidence:** One sourced fact or data point, with an understandable caption and citation.
4. **Perspectives:** Show that households, schools, communities, and ecosystems can be affected differently; avoid blaming people.
5. **Action:** Offer one realistic discussion or conservation action, not a mandate.
6. **Think/verify:** Ask learners to inspect a source, raise a question, or plan what to check locally.

## Grade-band adaptation

| Band | Audience choice | Evidence task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K–2 | Read aloud, concrete objects, picture-supported vocabulary; no alarming scarcity claims. | Teacher narrates a simple source caption and asks “What do you notice?” |
| 3–5 | Guided inquiry with labeled diagrams and partner talk. | Learners connect a caption to one observation or question. |
| 6–8 | Evidence discussion with a claim/evidence/reasoning prompt. | Learners identify the source, its limits, and a missing local fact. |
| 9–12 | Civic argument with multiple stakeholder perspectives. | Learners weigh a claim against source quality and propose a verifiable next step. |

## Non-negotiables

- Six slides only; use large type, high contrast, plain language, and one main idea per slide.
- Record source, license/permission, claim, and verification date for every factual visual or statement.
- Write alt text that communicates the instructional meaning, not merely colors or layout.
- Captions must distinguish a fact from an illustration, estimate, or question.
- Reject decorative images that mislead, stereotypes, fear-based visuals, and unsupported AI output.
