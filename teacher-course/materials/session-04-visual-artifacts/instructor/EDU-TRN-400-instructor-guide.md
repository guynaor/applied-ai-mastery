# EDU-TRN-400 — Instructor Guide

## Look-fors

- The deck has exactly six purposeful slides, not six text-heavy pages.
- Participants identify source scope and local uncertainty.
- Alt text communicates information; captions make claims legible.
- Adaptation changes the learning move, not only reading level.

## Coaching questions

- “What could a learner wrongly infer from this image?”
- “Whose experience is missing from this action slide?”
- “Could a student access the point without seeing color or hearing narration?”
- “For a high-school civic argument, where is the counterevidence or limitation?”

## Common corrections

Do not accept an AI-generated image as evidence. Do not accept “source: internet.” For K–2, replace abstract policy claims with observable questions; for 9–12, retain complexity but require source evaluation. Do not ask children to report household water use.
