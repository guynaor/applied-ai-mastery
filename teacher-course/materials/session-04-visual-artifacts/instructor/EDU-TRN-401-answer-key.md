# EDU-TRN-401 — Answer Key and Exemplar Decisions

There is no single deck design. Strong work makes these decisions:

- Slide 2 labels a water-cycle drawing as a **generic model** and holds the local supply route for teacher verification.
- Slide 3 uses a dated, traceable source and captions its scope. A school observation is not converted into a district-wide statistic.
- The K–2 version uses a read-aloud, picture vocabulary, and teacher-mediated source talk. The 3–5 version asks guided questions. The 6–8 version asks claim/evidence/reasoning. The 9–12 version compares stakeholder claims and evidence limits.
- Useful alt text: “Diagram uses arrows between cloud, rain, stream, and tap to show a simplified cycle; it is not a map of our town.”
- Reject an image of a dry, cracked field captioned “We are running out of water” if it has no local source, may induce fear, and overstates the claim. Replace it with a sourced local question or a generic diagram marked as illustrative.

A correct critique identifies a specific risk and a concrete revision, not merely “make it clearer.”
