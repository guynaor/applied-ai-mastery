# EDU-TRN-500 — Step-by-Step Lesson Script

**Time:** 80 minutes. **Outcome:** participants turn constraints into a realistic 10-day unit and locate their own decision points.

1. **0–10 min — Launch.** Say: “Constraints are not defects in a plan; they are the conditions that make it teachable.” Read the unit brief.
2. **10–22 min — Decode.** In pairs, annotate the constraints CSV. Ask them to circle the 35-minute day, shared materials, accommodations, assessment dates, and contingencies.
3. **22–48 min — Sequence.** Participants complete Days 2–10. Require one formative check before Day 6 and a contingency for outdoor work or technology failure.
4. **48–63 min — Differentiate.** Model the difference between lowering a task and changing access. Participants complete the differentiation plan.
5. **63–73 min — Communicate.** Draft the family calendar. Ask: “Does this invite partnership without collecting household data or assigning families teaching work?”
6. **73–80 min — Review.** Partners audit checkpoints for Days 3, 6, and 9. Exit ticket: name one decision only a teacher can make after reviewing learner evidence.
