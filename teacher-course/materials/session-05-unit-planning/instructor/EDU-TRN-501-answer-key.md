# EDU-TRN-501 — Answer Key and Exemplar Decisions

Strong sequences do not have one fixed order, but they include:

- Days 1–2: launch and low-risk model/observation; Day 3: quick check that informs Day 4.
- Day 4: compare sources/perspectives; Day 5: 35-minute synthesis, not the basin investigation.
- Day 6: teacher conference and revision; Days 7–9: communication product, peer accessibility check, and revision; Day 10: 70-minute sharing/reflection.
- If outdoor work is cancelled, learners inspect teacher-provided photos/data and identify the limitation. If a tablet fails, a paper data table and partner role keep the evidence task intact.
- Appropriate supports include accessible headings/alt text, home-language preview where available, visual schedule, and a dry role. They are confirmed with learners/staff rather than presumed.
- Family communication is optional, translated/plain-language when available, and does not ask for household water-use reporting.

The teacher uses Day 3/6/9 evidence to adjust support and pacing; no automated score determines a grade or next placement.
