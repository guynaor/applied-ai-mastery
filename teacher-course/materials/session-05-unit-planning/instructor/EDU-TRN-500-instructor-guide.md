# EDU-TRN-500 — Instructor Guide

## Facilitation priorities

Insist on a sequence that fits 50/35/70-minute periods. Strong plans use the shorter Day 5 for synthesis, not a new materials-heavy investigation. Require a paper/offline alternative and a dry simulation when water is restricted.

## Ask

- “Which objective is protected when time shrinks?”
- “What will you learn from Day 3 evidence, and what could you change on Day 4?”
- “How does this support preserve the intellectual task?”
- “What must be verified locally before this family note is sent?”

## Guardrails

Do not accept generic “differentiate” language without a concrete access route. Do not accept an AI-generated calendar as approved communication. No plan may automate grades, grouping, behavior, placement, or intervention decisions. Teacher checkpoints must name evidence and a possible human action.
