# EDU-OPS-504 — Differentiation Plan

## Central thinking

All learners will use observations and sources to explain one water-system relationship and communicate a responsible, evidence-aware action or question.

| Learner access need or strength | Planned support/choice | Same central thinking remains because… | Teacher check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K–2 / early readers | Read-aloud, picture sequence, oral rehearsal, drawing plus dictated caption. | Learner still connects evidence to an explanation. | Check vocabulary and image interpretation. |
| 3–5 | Guided data table, sentence stems, partner investigator roles. | Learner still interprets a source and observation. | Listen for evidence language. |
| 6–8 | Claim/evidence/reasoning organizer and source-limitation prompt. | Learner still evaluates evidence and revises a claim. | Conference after Day 6. |
| 9–12 | Stakeholder evidence set and civic argument with counterclaim. | Learner still makes a warranted, sourced argument. | Verify counterclaim and citation quality. |
| Screen-reader, multilingual, sensory, or participation variation | Headings/alt text, accessible digital text, home-language preview where available, visual schedule, dry/tactile-safe role, speech/drawing/writing response. | Access route changes; evidence reasoning does not. | Confirm supports with learner/staff, not assumptions. |

## Adjustment protocol

At checkpoints, record observed evidence, the support to continue/change, and why. Never use AI to determine grades, interventions, behavior consequences, or placement.
