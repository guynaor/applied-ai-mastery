# EDU-OPS-501 — Two-Week Water Unit Brief

Plan a fictional two-week **Water in Our Shared World** unit for one grade band. It connects science, literacy, mathematics/data, and civic communication. Select locally applicable standards only after the teacher checks them.

## Required outcomes

- A feasible 10-school-day sequence using the supplied constraints.
- At least one observation/investigation, one source-evaluation moment, one data representation, and one learner communication product.
- Formative checks before the final assessment; no AI-generated grade or placement decision.
- Differentiation that changes access, participation, and expression without lowering the central thinking.
- A family-calendar draft that invites optional conversation and names materials/events without requesting private household data.

## Teacher checkpoints

Before Day 1, verify standards, allergies/safety, access needs, source fit, and family translation needs. At the end of Days 3, 6, and 9, inspect learner evidence and adjust grouping, supports, pacing, or assessment. The teacher—not an automated system—makes every adjustment.

Use fictional examples only. Do not upload student work or personally identifiable data to an AI tool.
