# EDU-TRN-101 — River Systems Context Cards

All scenarios are fictional.

## K–2: Where does rain go?

Objective: learners sequence rain, stream, river, and sea using picture cards. Constraints: 25 minutes; read-aloud plus partner talk; bilingual picture labels; no independent web search; tactile cards for a learner with low vision.

## 3–5: How does a watershed connect places?

Objective: learners use a simple map to explain how water moves downhill. Constraints: 40 minutes; four map copies only; vocabulary support for multilingual learners; exit drawing with labels.

## 6–8: Why can one upstream choice affect a river?

Objective: learners cite two pieces of map/data evidence in a claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph. Constraints: 48 minutes; mixed reading levels; no claim about the local river without a source; paper alternative to slides.

## 9–12: Who should protect a shared watershed?

Objective: learners evaluate competing stakeholders and write a sourced civic recommendation. Constraints: 50 minutes; distinguish evidence from values; accessible digital and print sources; teacher verifies current local policy before use.
