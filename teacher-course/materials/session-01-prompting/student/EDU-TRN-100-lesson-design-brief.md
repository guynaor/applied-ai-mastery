# EDU-TRN-100 — River Systems Lesson-Design Brief

## Mission

Create a teacher-reviewed 35–50 minute River Systems inquiry lesson. Choose one context card, then ask an AI tool for a draft using the RISEN template. Revise the draft yourself.

## Required outcome

Submit a learning objective, opening, investigation, formative check, accessible materials plan, and closing. Cite the exact constraints you honored. Explain which standards language and claims you verified locally; district standards vary.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Do not use real student information. Do not let AI decide grouping, accommodations, grades, behavior responses, or intervention. The teacher decides whether the lesson is accurate and appropriate.

## Evidence to retain

Keep your selected context card, original prompt, output comparison, revised plan, verification notes, privacy check, and one next-step reflection.
