# EDU-TRN-100 — Instructor Guide

Success is a constrained, reviewable lesson draft—not fluent prose. Look for a measurable objective, a feasible sequence, a planned formative check, access supports tied to the card, and explicit flags for standards/local facts. Invite multiple valid designs: K–2 may use sequencing cards, 3–5 a map, 6–8 CER, and 9–12 stakeholder analysis.

Intervene when participants paste real classroom records, accept an invented standard, or treat AI grouping/behavior suggestions as decisions. Ask: “What evidence would make this safe to teach tomorrow?”
