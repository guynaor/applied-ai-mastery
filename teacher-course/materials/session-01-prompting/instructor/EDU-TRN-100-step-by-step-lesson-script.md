# EDU-TRN-100 — Step-by-Step Lesson Script

## Preparation

Provide the four context cards and keep the scenario fictional. Ask participants to select a band based on instructional fit, not a perceived learner label.

## 0–10 minutes: frame the boundary

Say: “AI can produce a draft. It cannot know our standards, learners, or classroom context.” Model rejecting “make it engaging” as an insufficient requirement.

## 10–25 minutes: draft and compare

Participants complete RISEN and compare it with the vague prompt. Pause them to identify an invented standard, inaccessible activity, or missing material constraint.

## 25–40 minutes: teacher revision

Participants revise one output and name the local verification required. Collect the comparison table.

## Debrief

Ask which part required teacher judgment. Confirm no real learner data was used.
