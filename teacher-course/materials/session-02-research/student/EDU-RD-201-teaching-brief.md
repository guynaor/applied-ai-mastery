# EDU-RD-201 — Teaching Research Brief

## Decision question

Should fictional Cedar Ridge School pilot a short watershed field-observation activity as part of its River Systems unit?

Read the four supplied sources. Build an evidence matrix that distinguishes district guidance, a vendor claim, a classroom observation, and a research summary. Your memo must recommend: safe to try, must check locally, and not established.

## K–12 transfer

Explain how the activity would differ for K–2 (guided noticing), 3–5 (mapped observation), 6–8 (evidence collection), or 9–12 (method/ethics critique). Do not claim the sources prove outcomes for every grade.

## Boundary

Sources and school are fictional. No individual learner data, behavior records, or family information may be used.
