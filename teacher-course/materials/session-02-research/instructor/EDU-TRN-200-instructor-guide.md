# EDU-TRN-200 — Instructor Guide

Emphasize that EDU-SRC-201 is useful for local conditions, EDU-SRC-204 offers qualified research context, EDU-SRC-203 is a single contextual observation, and EDU-SRC-202 is an unsupported vendor assertion. Do not let participants convert “can support engagement” into a guaranteed achievement claim.

Strong K–12 transfer keeps the evidence claim constant while changing the method: supported noticing for K–2, mapping for 3–5, structured evidence for 6–8, and methodological/civic critique for 9–12.
