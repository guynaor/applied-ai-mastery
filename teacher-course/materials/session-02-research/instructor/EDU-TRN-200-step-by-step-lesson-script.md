# EDU-TRN-200 — Step-by-Step Lesson Script

## 0–8 minutes

Introduce the decision question. Say that a source type is not a quality ranking by itself; purpose, method, and limits matter.

## 8–25 minutes

Teams annotate sources and complete the matrix. Ask them to highlight absolute language in the vendor claim and missing comparison/method information in the observation.

## 25–40 minutes

Draft a bounded memo. Require “safe to try,” “check locally,” and “not established” headings.

## Exit check

Each participant names one claim they refused to make and confirms no real learner data was used.
