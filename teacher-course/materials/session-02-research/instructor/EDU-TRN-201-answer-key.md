# EDU-TRN-201 — Answer Key

A defensible memo may recommend a small, teacher-designed pilot only after local safety, access, permissions, and curriculum fit are confirmed. The district guidance supports conditions, not learning impact. The research summary supports a cautious possibility, not a promised result. The observation can generate questions but cannot establish effectiveness. The vendor claim is not supported by supplied evidence.

Accept alternatives that accurately express uncertainty and retain local teacher review.
