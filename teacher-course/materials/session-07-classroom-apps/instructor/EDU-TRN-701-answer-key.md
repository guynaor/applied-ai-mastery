# EDU-TRN-701 — Answer Key

## Evidence of a sound submission

- The hub stays in one local file with embedded CSS/JavaScript and no dependency, form, analytics, account, cookie, or network request.
- The disclosure control is a native button with an associated region and `aria-expanded`; keyboard focus is visible.
- The watershed diagram uses alt text that names the blue river path and its directional relationship, rather than “image” or a filename.
- The selected grade band changes language or cognitive demand: K–2 needs adult-supported concise steps; 6–8 needs evidence discussion; 9–12 can evaluate civic claims.
- The inventory identifies materials as fictional/public and the teacher verifies suitability before local use.

## Common revisions

Remove a generic “click here” link, improve it to name the resource, and keep focus visible. If learners propose a feedback form or progress tracker, reject it: that would collect data and exceed the resource-hub scope.
