# EDU-TRN-700 — Instructor Guide

## Facilitation priorities

Treat the app as a learning artifact, not a product demo. Require learners to test interaction without a mouse and to make concrete decisions about language and images for a selected grade band. The hub must remain local and dependency-free.

## Ask

- “What does this diagram communicate to a learner who cannot see it?”
- “Which phrase would overload a second grader? Which evidence task would underchallenge a high-school learner?”
- “Where could data leave this page? How do you know it does not?”
- “What must the teacher verify before a resource is shared?”

## Guardrails

Reject forms, analytics, remote scripts, logins, tracking, student names, saved answers, grade features, and behavior features. Do not accept color-only instructions, decorative alt text for an informative image, or accessibility claims without a test record. A technically polished hub that is unsuitable for the selected age band does not meet the mission.
